Bipolar transistors (Ebers-Moll transport model with Early effect, `Q` lines, `NPN`/`PNP` models) and MOSFETs (SPICE level 1 with body effect, `M` lines, `NMOS`/`PMOS` models). Terminal currents are read with `current("Q1:b")`, power sums over every terminal, and schematics draw both symbols. New guide: Transistors (EN/FR).
